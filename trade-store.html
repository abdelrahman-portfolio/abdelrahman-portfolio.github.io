THE CAMPAIGN

Launching SKYWORTH

A product launch campaign introducing SKYWORTH's new power bank and charger range for Trade House, told through six short-form reels.

MY ROLE

From Script to Screen

I wrote six scripts built around real problems people face, such as not knowing that power banks can't be carried on a flight, and showed how SKYWORTH solves them. I prepared and supervised the shoot, then worked closely with the video editor to shape the final look of each video.
